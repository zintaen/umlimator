## What it shows {#shows}

<p>In the order you find them in the picture:</p>

<ol class="ex-shows">
<li>People in C4's person shape, with their description (Shopper, Shop staff)</li>
<li>The subject's boundary, a dashed box titled <code>Bookshop [Software System]</code>, holding its containers</li>
<li>Containers with C4's three lines: the name, <code>[Container: technology]</code> and the description</li>
<li>Container shapes chosen by <code>shape</code>: a phone (Mobile app), a browser (Storefront), a terminal (Stock import), a robot (Order worker), a folder (Cover images), a cylinder (Database), a pipe (Order queue)</li>
<li>A container in the default box (Shop API)</li>
<li>Relationships drawn solid with an open arrow, labelled <code>description [technology]</code></li>
<li>A relationship with no technology, its description alone (Load price lists with)</li>
<li>A label cut with an ellipsis at 280 px (Reads the catalogue and places orders through [JSO...])</li>
<li>A view that draws only the relationships its <code>edges</code> lists (the email to the shopper and the worker's database write are left out)</li>
<li>An implied relationship, dashed: Shop API to Email service, standing for Accounts and sign-in's password reset emails</li>
<li>External software systems in grey outside the boundary (Payment provider, Email service, Courier)</li>
<li>A note linked by a dashed line to a container (Order worker)</li>
</ol>
