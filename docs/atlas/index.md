# Atlases

An atlas is one model of a whole business in many views of many diagram types, joined by cards that open them. Each opens on a map, its first layer: the [Bookshop atlas's overview](./bookshop.md#view-overview) is the map's showcase, with a checklist of everything a map draws. On any of them you can pan, zoom, select and go into the cards that stack.
