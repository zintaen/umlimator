Diagram editor: UI draft

A drawing of the target, not a build. It follows the decisions taken: diagrams lay themselves out and a user pins what matters; the host owns the model; every panel is a piece a host may turn on around its own page, as the views list and details panel are today. Labels and positions are illustrative.

Drag ↕ or press Alt + ↑ ↓ to move a message in time · drag between two lifelines to add one · numbers, activation bars and the fragment's frame are drawn from the order, never stored

Nothing here is placed by hand: lifelines are columns in the order given, messages are rows in time order, and a frame covers the messages that name its operand. Moving a message is a change of order, which is meaning.
