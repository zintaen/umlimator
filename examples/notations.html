Card shapes

A map draws each card as the shape its kind has: a person (person, user), a robot (agent), a browser app (frontend), a phone (mobile), a terminal (cli), a pipe (queue), a folder (module, package), a host (cloud), a cylinder (database), a page (file) and a hexagon (a hook), and a rounded box where no shape says more. A card's own shape wins: here, the back office is a window. Each shape keeps to its card's box, so the layout is the same as a box's.
