Card shapes

A notation draws each card as a shape: box (the default, every card a rounded box), c4 (people, browser apps, phones, databases, queues and files as the C4 model's tools draw them) or uml (UML 2.5's actor, node, artifact, package and data store, with UML's component icon on a box). A card's own shape wins in every notation: here, the back office is a window. Each shape keeps to its card's box, so the layout is the same in all three.
