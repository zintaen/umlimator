In an app

PlaygroundEdit a model and see the map, its problems (validate()) and its layout check (check()); share it by link.
A host pagerender() with a details panel, breadcrumbs, views loaded when opened, SVG and PNG export, themes and the minimap.
Live trafficoverlay() every second: flow as fast as the traffic, errors that pulse, slow lines that glow, lag on each card; the model never changes.
Ask the mapQuestions answered with words, the paths or reach they are about lit, and a tour; the view in sentences (describe()) for an AI model.
Trace replayAn OpenTelemetry trace played on the map: each call along its line at its time, with its duration.
EditingThe map emits change events; the host applies them and keeps an undo history.
In Nodelayout() without a browser, as a check a host can run in CI on maps it generates.
